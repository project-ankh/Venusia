Questions, Answered

Honest answers before you decide.

Are spells guaranteed to work?

No. I never promise outcomes. A spell is a focused ritual of energy and intention. Results depend on timing, circumstance and the free will of everyone involved.

How long until I see results?

It varies. Some people notice shifts within days, others over a few weeks or a full moon cycle. Some may see nothing.

Can you make a specific person love me?

I cannot override anyone's free will. Love Attraction and Love Binding work on energy and connection, and the outcome is never certain.

Do I need to be present during the ritual?

No. I perform the ritual myself and update you afterwards. You may be asked for a name, intention or a small detail.

What if it doesn't work?

Payment is for the ritual performed, not the result. We can talk about what may have blocked it and whether another approach could help.

Is a reading the same as a spell?

No. Readings give insight and guidance. Spells are rituals meant to influence energy. Many people start with a reading.

Can I request a custom ritual?

Yes. Custom Rituals start at ₹777 and the price depends on the work involved.

Do readings replace professional advice?

No. Please see qualified professionals for medical, mental health, legal or financial matters.
